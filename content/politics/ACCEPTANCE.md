### Relation ownership

Learner-visible relation text is content, not connector chrome. Directed transitions resolve explicitly as:

```text

ORDER_ONLY       → Surface Mapping owns direction/order only
```

`ORDER_ONLY` is not permission for UI to synthesize a label such as “下一步”. The renderer may draw direction faithfully but must not create semantic prose.

Independent relations remain `RELATION_SET`; peer knowledge remains `PARALLEL_SET`; comparison, hierarchy and timeline each retain their explicit primitive instead of being flattened into a generic graph.

### State-payload exclusivity

For a mapped PASS owner, learner-facing payload for a mapped state comes only from the resolved `surfacePlan.states[state]`.

Runtime / UI may own timing, triggers, interaction shells and responsive presentation. It may not repopulate learner content from legacy hierarchy/handoff/closure/next fields, raw teaching fields, inferred field names, Projection shape names, or rendered DOM.

Repair is single-writer at the learner-content layer: `PoliticsRepairEnhancer` consumes the resolved `REPAIR` state for mapped units. Question/Evidence logic still owns the repair trigger and durable Evidence metadata; it does not author a parallel repair explanation.
