# Politics Analysis Diagnostic Rubric

Status: **ACTIVE DIAGNOSTIC RUBRIC — not a precise exam-score converter**

Role:
> Diagnose Politics Analysis capability and select the smallest useful Repair without pretending that one Chat/model rating equals the real exam score.

This owner does not define Current political content or annual exact wording.

---

## 1. Measurement boundary

The rubric may judge whether an answer is structurally broken, partial, independently usable, or usable under time.

It may not by itself claim:
- an exact per-question exam score;
- an exact Analysis /50 score;
- an exact Politics total;
- a narrow Protect-70 / Push-75 score band.

Inter-rater agreement is not sufficient if both raters share the same wrong rubric.

---

## 2. Rating states

- **0 — BROKEN**: a material capability failure.
- **1 — PARTIAL**: right direction but incomplete, generic or fragile.
- **2 — USABLE**: independently usable for the tested task.
- **U — UNKNOWN**: not tested, contaminated, ambiguous, or blocked by missing current-year source.

These are diagnostic evidence states, not mastery scores.

---

## 3. D1 — Prompt interpretation

Question: Did the learner answer the relation actually asked?

0 BROKEN:
- wrong relation or topic dump;
- misses cause / relation / evaluation / measure / significance task geometry.

1 PARTIAL:
- broad topic correct, requested relation blurred.

2 USABLE:
- requested relation is identified correctly before content generation.

U:
- prompt itself is ambiguous or unvalidated.

Repair: Prompt Interpretation / Material Segmentation.

---

## 4. D2 — Principle / political-position selection

Question: Did the learner choose the smallest valid owner?

0 BROKEN:
- wrong owner;
- adjacent principle selected;
- all-purpose theory dump.

1 PARTIAL:
- broadly relevant owner but hierarchy is incomplete or competitor is not excluded.

2 USABLE:
- correct owner selected and irrelevant neighbors excluded.

U:
- required current-year owner is source-blocked.

Repair: Principle Retrieval / competing-principle Stress.

---

## 5. D3 — Skeleton / logical coverage

Question: Does the answer contain the necessary 3–5 moves in a coherent order?

0 BROKEN:
- decisive move missing;
- unordered list;
- material contradiction.

1 PARTIAL:
- core structure exists but one important link is missing or redundant.

2 USABLE:
- compact, relevant, ordered structure covers the required reasoning.

U:
- accepted answer structure is not source-grounded enough to judge.

Repair: Skeleton.

---

## 6. D4 — Material binding

Question: Does each major move actually connect to the supplied material?

0 BROKEN:
- template could fit many unrelated materials;
- almost no concrete material link.

1 PARTIAL:
- some real binding, but one or more major claims remain generic.

2 USABLE:
- major answer moves explicitly use decisive material cues.

U:
- material itself is insufficient or ambiguous.

Hard rule:
> A memorized correct skeleton with BROKEN material binding is not Analysis Secure.

Repair: Material Segmentation / Material Binding.

---

## 7. D5 — Formulation legitimacy / precision

Question: Is the wording source-legitimate and precise enough for this task?

0 BROKEN:
- factual/source error;
- wrong actor/identity/relation;
- unsupported current-year wording;
- materially distorted formulation.

1 PARTIAL:
- concept usable but wording is vague or not yet exact enough.

2 USABLE:
- accepted source supports the formulation and precision is sufficient.

U:
- high-delta current-year wording is not yet available/validated.

Historical wording may support structure but cannot receive 2 USABLE for current-year exactness without current source authorization.

Repair: source-grounded Formulation Retrieval / current-year overlay.

---

## 8. D6 — Completion / answer density

Question: Does the final answer turn the structure into a usable response without filler?

0 BROKEN:
- incomplete;
- decisive relevant part absent;
- mostly filler.

1 PARTIAL:
- mostly complete but inefficient, underdeveloped or redundant.

2 USABLE:
- sufficiently complete, compact and readable.

U:
- full prose was not requested/tested.

Repair: Complete Analysis delivery.

---

## 9. D7 — Timed delivery / modality

Question: Can the learner preserve the above under realistic time and delivery conditions?

0 BROKEN:
- unfinished;
- material quality collapse;
- handwriting/paper cost changes performance materially.

1 PARTIAL:
- completes with meaningful pacing or quality loss.

2 USABLE:
- phase-appropriate complete delivery under tested time/modality.

U:
- no timed/paper evidence.

Repair: Timed Analysis / whole-paper execution.

---

## 10. Task-depth applicability

IDENTIFY: rate D1–D2 only.
SEGMENTATION: rate segmentation evidence; do not pretend full output was tested.
SKELETON: rate D1–D3; rate D4 only when binding is explicitly required.
MATERIAL BINDING: rate D1–D4.
FORMULATION RETRIEVAL: rate D5.
COMPLETE ANALYSIS: rate D1–D6.
TIMED COMPLETE ANALYSIS: rate D1–D7.

Untested dimensions stay U.

---

## 11. Control rules

Build / Repair when:
- a Protect-70-critical tested dimension is 0 BROKEN;
- the same 1 PARTIAL mechanism recurs;
- source revision invalidates D5.

Verify after Repair with a different material/task identity.
Same-prompt rewrite is Repair evidence only, not fresh Transfer.

Maintain only when:
- representative independent evidence exists;
- no relevant BROKEN state remains;
- repeated PARTIAL mechanism has stopped;
- timed/modality evidence exists when phase-relevant.

Reopen on:
- newer contradictory evidence;
- source revision;
- current-year fresh transfer drop;
- timed/whole-paper contradiction.

---

## 12. Protect-70 safety

The Analysis 30+ path may not be called healthy while a material unresolved failure remains in:
- D1 on representative unseen prompts;
- D2 across representative domains;
- D3 on important task families;
- D4 despite a correct skeleton;
- D5 when exact current-year wording is required;
- D6 on representative complete-output work;
- D7 once timed delivery is phase-relevant.

Early phase may legitimately report:
> STRUCTURAL_PATH_HEALTHY / FINAL_SCORE_CONFIDENCE_WIDE

when D1–D4 are strong but D5/D7/current-year evidence remains UNKNOWN.

---

## 13. Independent second review

Require a second independent review when a subjective rating would materially change:
- Protect-70 vs at-risk status;
- a major allocation change;
- whether Analysis enters Maintenance;
- a narrow current-year score claim.

Procedure:
1. reviewer sees task, learner answer, accepted rubric/source and relevant current-year source;
2. reviewer should not first read the original rating/rationale when practical;
3. reviewer rates dimensions independently;
4. disagreement is reconciled by evidence, not averaging;
5. unresolved material disagreement becomes UNKNOWN / wider confidence.

A repairer may self-check, but a material scoring-rule change needs fresh independent revalidation before high-confidence use.

---

## 14. Systematic-bias attacks

Attack at least:
- rewarding long answers regardless of material fit;
- rewarding template resemblance;
- penalizing concise but complete answers;
- over-rewarding exact wording while missing wrong logic;
- accepting correct logic with unsupported current-year wording;
- two raters sharing one biased answer key;
- historical scaffold being mistaken for current-year exactness;
- many political terms being mistaken for task fulfillment;
- different valid wording being punished despite correct structure/binding.

---

## 15. Score-conversion boundary

Example diagnostic vector:

    D1 2
    D2 2
    D3 1
    D4 0
    D5 U
    D6 U
    D7 U

Valid conclusion:
> material binding is the real current blocker; exact current-year wording and timed delivery remain untested.

Invalid conclusion from this vector alone:
> this is exactly X/10 or Analysis is exactly Y/50.

Numeric score conversion remains later current-year calibration work.

---

## 16. Minimal evidence identity

Preserve:
- task_id and task_revision;
- source_basis and current-year status;
- learner attempt identity;
- exposure state;
- requested depth;
- D1–D7 ratings only where tested;
- rater identity/method;
- rating timestamp;
- Repair link;
- independent second-review status;
- contamination / UNKNOWN notes.

Private learner answers remain private learner truth. Shared Current stores reusable rubric/task semantics only.
