### O1 — Relation / model reconstruction

**Exam ability**
Understand why a political proposition is true/false and how concepts relate.

**Failure**
Recognition without causal/structural understanding; source rereading required for every variation.

**Material**
Chengfeng NU + Current teaching bridge/compression; Suyi only as framework/reference support.

Maintain when later questions remain stable. Reopen only on repeated relation-level errors or new source change.

## 3. Score-path gates

### Protect-70 Objective gate

Do not call the Objective 40+ path healthy if any material condition remains:

- large unlearned Current scope;
- systematic multiple-choice boundary failure;
- exact-memory holes repeatedly causing losses;
- W/U root causes growing faster than repair;
- fresh/current-year transfer materially worse than training performance;
- timed execution failure.

### Protect-70 Analysis gate

Do not call the Analysis 30+ path healthy if:

- unseen material cannot be mapped to a principle;
- skeletons are incomplete;
- material binding remains generic;
- current-year exact wording is required but not yet source-grounded;
- complete answers fail under realistic time.

### Push-75 gate

Push-75 capacity becomes elastic only after Protect-70 risks are bounded.

High-value upside may include:

- multiple-choice variance reduction;
- exact current-year formulation;
- stronger material binding;
- denser complete answers;
- better timed completion.

If an extra Politics hour does not materially protect 70 or improve the 75 path, it should compete normally against Xizong/English.

## 5. Dynamic control rules

Politics does not use a fixed D1/D3/D7 cadence.

Conceptual control states:

```text
REACTIVATE
→ BUILD
→ VERIFY
→ STABILIZE
→ MAINTAIN
→ ELASTIC
```

### Reduce practice when

- representative evidence exists;
- W/U mechanism no longer recurs;
- delayed recall remains stable;
- changed-context performance is stable;
- new practice produces almost no actionable information.

### Increase practice when

- a mechanism error recurs;
- multiple-choice is materially weaker than single-choice;
- Memory relapses;
- fresh/current-year transfer falls below training performance;
- material binding or timed output fails;
- whole-paper evidence contradicts local stability.

### False-Unstable guard

A Secure/Maintain capability may not return to heavy Build merely because more material exists. Require newer contradictory evidence or an explicit reopen trigger.
