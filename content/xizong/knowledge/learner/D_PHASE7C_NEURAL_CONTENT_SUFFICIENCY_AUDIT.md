## 1｜Audit question

For every accepted neural Logic Group:

> after its accepted Source contact, does the canonical Block Core provide enough learner-facing material to retrieve, check, repair and close the intended local model without requiring a second continuous KianOS Lecture?

Tests:

1. LG causal / discrimination job is represented by its member KPs;
2. member KPs are sufficient for the accepted closure;
3. prompts/identity provide retrieval scaffolding without making reconstruction pointless;
4. Detailed Expansion is sufficient as Core check / repair / reference;
5. precision/confusable/boundary support exists where materially needed;
6. visual-required cognition delegates decisive spatial formation to original Source visuals;
7. Source gaps/conflicts remain explicit;
8. closure does not depend on a false prior-Block completion assumption.

Prompt-leakage threshold used here is deliberately conservative: a descriptive title or mnemonic is not rejected merely for being informative. A defect is recorded when the **pre-Reveal identity/prompt essentially states the accepted LG closure itself**, especially for a singleton LG where no further reconstruction remains.
