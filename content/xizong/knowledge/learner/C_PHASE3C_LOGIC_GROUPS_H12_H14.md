## C-H14-LG01｜第一问永远是谁攻击谁：排斥 vs GVHD

- **KP:** 01–03, 12
- **Cognitive job:** `DIRECTION_TIMING + DISCRIMINATION`
- **Goal:** separate recipient→graft rejection from graft immune cells→recipient GVHD, connect HLA mismatch to rejection intensity and state the two conditions for GVHD; include transfusion-related GVHD as the same attack direction from a different source.
- **Closure:** distinguish organ rejection, ordinary transfusion hemolysis, bone-marrow GVHD and transfusion-GVHD purely by attacker/target/source before using any timing or pathology clue.

## C-H14-LG02｜超急性：预存抗体造成分钟—小时血管灾难
