## 3｜Fresh adversarial attacks

### 3.1 Learner-order attack — H6 bleeding localization vs ITP

**Attack:** ITP-first LG retrieval might make ITP the first cognitive model and postpone bleeding localization.

**Result:** challenge retained after attack, not a red point.

Canonical H6 itself requires the bleeding localization coordinate before detailed disease reasoning. The accepted Learning design satisfies this through **Block Orientation before continuous Source contact**: first establish superficial/mucosal vs deep/joint/muscle vs delayed rebleed and the initial Plt/PT/APTT/TT coordinate, then preserve the original Lecture's continuous order. Reordering the Lecture or stable KP identity is unnecessary and would damage Source continuity.

### 3.2 Grouping attack — H9 acute leukemia

**Attack:** morphology, cytochemistry, flow and genetics could have been compressed into a table.

**Result:** attack failed.

The candidate keeps distinct evidence roles: acute/blast identity, marrow/morphology/cytochemistry, flow lineage, genetic subtype/prognosis, APL/urgent integration, treatment and CR/MRD. The evidence stack survives rather than becoming one classification table.

### 3.3 Grouping attack — H11 lymphoma

**Attack:** subtype learning could have degraded into name–CD–translocation lists.

**Result:** attack failed.

The candidate preserves tissue architecture / biopsy / cell origin / behavior / stage as the organizing frame. Marker and chromosome information confirm or discriminate rather than independently owning diagnosis; T/NK heterogeneity is not forced into a fake common mechanism.

### 3.4 Source-boundary attack — H12

**Attack:** complete normal immunology might have been silently imported.

**Result:** attack failed.

H12 remains a bounded minimum language for hypersensitivity, effector direction and tolerance interface. It does not backfill a full complement/APC/cytokine/lymphocyte-development/tolerance course.

### 3.5 Evidence-role attack — H15–H19

**Attack:** rheumatology could have degraded into “see antibody → name disease.”

**Result:** attack failed.

The accepted logic preserves screening / specificity / activity / organ-evidence roles and syndrome/organ combinations. ANA, RF/CCP, SSA/SSB and ANCA do not become standalone diagnosis keys.

### 3.6 Infection-scope / ownership attacks — H20–H27
