## 4｜Boundary notes

### Endocrine-common signaling is allowed when endocrine classification itself is the tested construct

`2005N20`, `2006N17`, and `2012N21` are not admitted merely because a hormone name appears. They test hormone chemical class / receptor / intracellular signaling choice, which is exactly the Current endocrine-common substrate.

By contrast, generic receptor/pharmacology questions without an endocrine decision target remain outside B.

### Cross-organ outputs do not change endocrine ownership when the hormone physiology is the decision variable

Examples:

- GH excess producing hyperglycemia;
- GC permissive effects on vessels;
- PTH-driven Ca/P regulation;
- insulin effects on glucose handling.

These remain B endocrine because the question asks the hormone's physiology. Conversely, when the question asks renal concentration, autonomic output or reproductive physiology, that organ/control owner wins.
