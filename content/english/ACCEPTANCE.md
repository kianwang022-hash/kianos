## Acceptance boundaries

- Source inventory or recovered bytes alone do not establish learner readiness.
- Build/browser/CI success supports only the claim it actually tests.
- Lookup success is not learner evidence.
- Correctness under unknown exposure/timing is not automatically stable transfer.
- Module PASS does not automatically prove unrelated whole-paper, cross-owner or disaster-recovery behavior.
- Real learner progress remains private learner truth, never repository Current.
