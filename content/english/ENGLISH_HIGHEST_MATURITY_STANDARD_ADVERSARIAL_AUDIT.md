# English Highest Maturity Standard — English-specific Adversarial Audit

Status: **CANDIDATE SELF-ATTACK · DO NOT FREEZE**

Target:
`content/english/ENGLISH_HIGHEST_MATURITY_STANDARD.md`

Purpose:

> Construct English-specific all-green failure scenarios before the standard is allowed to control implementation.

---

## E1｜Fresh-material capital can be wasted while every rule looks correct — MATERIAL

Scenario:

- old English-I papers are already substantially exposed;
- the system uses the cleanest remaining official material for frequent calibration;
- each individual use is justified;
- by 10/20 or 11/15 no high-value low-contamination integrated material remains.

Failure:

> evidence quality improves locally while future formal calibration capability is destroyed.

Required addition:

- fresh / least-contaminated material is a scarce **capital budget**;
- use must be allocated by value of information and future Gate need;
- engineering validation never consumes protected learner capital;
- the system must preserve enough high-value integrated evidence for later Gates where feasible.

---

## E2｜Objective 60/60 can create an impossible proof burden — MATERIAL

Scenario:

- Objective is treated as a hard capability target;
- old official questions are contaminated;
- system keeps demanding more fresh evidence before calling Objective Secure;
- synthetic cannot be score-equivalent;
- result is endless verification or excessive fresh-capital consumption.

Required clarification:

> 60/60 means **no known recurring predictable loss mechanism**, not infinite statistical proof that Kian will literally score 60 on every paper.

Secure Objective can be supported by:

- representative mechanism/form coverage;
- low-contamination performance;
- normal timing;
- later integrated Whole Paper evidence;
- explicit residual uncertainty.

Do not require unlimited fresh official samples.

---

## E3｜A total 85+ estimate can be falsely narrow even if every section estimate is individually honest — MATERIAL

Scenario:

- Objective exact score is known on one paper;
- Translation/Writing each receive plausible anchored ranges;
- total score simply adds section midpoints.

Failure:

- correlated fatigue / time competition / scorer variance is ignored;
- whole-paper score uncertainty is understated.

Required addition:

- total-score synthesis must preserve covariance / shared execution risk qualitatively;
- Whole Paper is the primary check on section independence assumptions;
- do not mechanically add independent section point estimates as if errors were uncorrelated.

---

## E4｜Writing can overfit topic/content while passing prompt-transfer checks — MATERIAL

Scenario:

- several synthetic prompts use similar abstract themes;
- Kian learns high-quality reusable paragraphs / arguments;
- different prompt IDs are technically “fresh”;
- performance looks stable.

Failure:

> the learner transfers memorized topic packages rather than generation ability.

Required addition:

- Big Writing Transfer bank must vary **semantic theme, visual relation and argument demand**, not only prompt wording;
- evaluation must inspect information generation / grounding rather than lexical novelty;
- repeated reusable sentences are allowed, but reusable whole-content packages must not be mistaken for generative capability.

---

## E5｜Translation can overfit reference style — MATERIAL

Scenario:

- Kian repeatedly compares against synthetic/reference translations;
- output converges toward a preferred stylistic pattern;
- fidelity appears high under the same evaluator.

Failure:

- acceptable alternative Chinese renderings are penalized;
- source meaning is subordinated to reference wording.

Required addition:

- scoring/repair judges proposition/relation preservation first;
- reference wording is non-unique;
- multi-valid-rendering attack is required in scoring validation.

---

## E6｜Lexical throughput can Goodhart English planning — MATERIAL

Scenario:

- Kian learns words very quickly;
- system observes high Coverage throughput and increasing card stability;
- English allocation remains high because lexical backlog is large.

Failure:

> the learner spends hours improving lexical catalog completion after downstream task error has stopped changing.

Required addition:

- Lexical Coverage scale cannot by itself justify English capacity;
- after core reactivation, marginal lexical work must be justified by downstream transfer, long-term compounding or a concrete production/recognition need;
- “7,946 Main Words” is content scope, not a required exam-cycle completion count.

---

## E7｜Strong browser productive evidence can still fail on paper — MATERIAL

Scenario:

- Translation/Writing are stable under typed output;
- Whole Paper browser execution passes;
- handwriting is much slower and increases language error / incomplete output.

Required addition:

- formal productive calibration must include handwriting/paper evidence before high-confidence 85+ claims;
- browser output remains valid cognitive evidence but has a modality penalty until calibrated.

---

## E8｜Assistance contamination is especially subtle after normal Chat discussion — MATERIAL

Scenario:

- Kian discusses a Writing idea / Translation sentence / Reading mechanism with Chat;
- later opens an otherwise unseen task of the same target mechanism;
- runtime marks the task “unassisted” because no on-page assistance button was used.

Failure:

> semantic assistance occurred outside the runtime but evidence is promoted as fully independent.

Required addition:

- learner/Chat may downgrade evidence independence when prior conversation materially cued the target mechanism/prompt;
- runtime facts are necessary but not sufficient to prove cognitive independence;
- uncertain assistance remains UNKNOWN rather than clean.

---

## E9｜Representative coverage can be source-biased — QUALITY

Scenario:

- Reading A representative mechanisms are covered only by synthetic;
- official low-contamination evidence covers mostly easier/detail questions.

Failure:

- coverage table looks complete, but difficulty/source interaction remains untested.

Required addition:

Representative coverage tracks at least:

- mechanism;
- task form;
- difficulty/stress;
- source/evidence class;
- timed/integrated context when relevant.

No need for a giant matrix UI; the acceptance audit must detect obvious empty cells.

---

## E10｜Whole Paper task order can be mistaken for immutable exam cognition — QUALITY

Scenario:

- runtime always uses official printed order;
- another order would materially protect Writing/Translation quality;
- system never tests it because “official order” is encoded as default.

Required addition:

- printed section order is Source truth;
- **execution order is a learner strategy variable** when exam rules permit;
- Whole Paper may test alternative task order if evidence suggests time/fatigue value;
- no order change is adopted without real execution evidence.

---

## E11｜External Reading can become an attractive distraction — QUALITY

Scenario:

- TPO/IELTS long-form reading is high quality and enjoyable;
- it improves general English;
- Kian's actual score bottleneck is Writing.

Failure:

> a useful asset receives low-ROI exam time because its intrinsic learning value is high.

Required addition:

External Reading only receives protected exam capacity when:

- it repairs a current reading/lexical/stamina need; or
- core 85+ path is secure and it wins Elastic ROI.

Intrinsic quality alone does not justify allocation.

---

## E12｜Synthetic executability is part of Material Closure — MATERIAL

Scenario:

- synthetic banks are semantically excellent;
- files and keys exist;
- current Website/Session Catalog cannot address them.

Failure:

> Chat has to manually paste tasks or improvise a training surface.

Required addition:

Any asset counted as **ready inventory** must be executable through an accepted learner path with:

- identity;
- answer/reference gating;
- evidence capture;
- exposure role.

Otherwise classify it PREPARED_NOT_EXECUTABLE rather than READY.

---

## E13｜Full-paper “three times” can become a ritual quota — QUALITY

Scenario:

- contract prior says approximately three full papers before stable execution;
- two highly representative full executions already show stable timing;
- or five runs still show instability.

Failure:

- exact count becomes ceremony.

Required clarification:

Whole-paper count is an evidence prior only. Secure requires enough representative integrated evidence, not a sacred number.

---

## E14｜Productive secure targets can themselves become fake precision — QUALITY

Scenario:

- planning says Translation 7–8 and Writing 21–23;
- those numbers become treated as exact thresholds before rubric calibration.

Required addition:

- productive planning bands are provisional until accepted scoring calibration;
- after calibration, Secure is tied to target viability + uncertainty, not a single magic cut score.

---

## E15｜English Forecast can ignore opportunity-cost regime shifts — MATERIAL

Scenario:

- early English ROI is high because Writing starts near zero;
- after rapid improvement, English remains scheduled at the same protected floor;
- Xizong becomes binding.

Required addition:

- English floor/target/ceiling are phase- and evidence-dependent;
- no permanent “2h/day English” obligation survives current cross-subject evidence;
- continuity may be protected with much less once stable.

# Round 2 — Current-product cross-boundary attack

## E16｜Whole-paper formal evidence can lose contamination/assistance context — MATERIAL

Current whole-paper runtime correctly seals exact section work and releases exact Objective scoring. But formal whole-paper evidence must also preserve or reconcile the **constituent material exposure and assistance status**.

All-green failure:

- paper score = 92;
- runtime identity / Seal / objective key are all correct;
- several constituent Reading/Cloze items were previously exposed or Chat-assisted;
- final score packet is interpreted as clean formal calibration.

Requirement:

A whole-paper score claim must state, per paper or constituent task as needed:

- prior exposure / unknown exposure;
- assistance;
- source revision;
- which parts are eligible for clean calibration;
- whether contamination widens the total score band.

Exact scoring correctness does not equal fresh-score validity.

---

## E17｜Bounded Daily Packet can hide long-horizon mechanism recurrence — MATERIAL

Current English packet design intentionally carries only bounded recent exact attempts plus fixed-size all-history task summaries. This is good for attention/performance, but it creates a compression risk.

All-green failure:

- a Reading inference defect recurred several times three weeks ago;
- recent 8 Reading attempts do not show it;
- history summary still reports only task-level problem counts;
- Fresh Chat concludes the mechanism is absent.

Requirement:

- bounded packets remain the default;
- they must preserve enough long-horizon signal to detect decision-relevant recurrence, or expose a trigger for a deeper targeted review/export;
- truncation must be visible;
- Chat must not infer “mechanism absent” from “not present in recent exact window”.

The solution must remain bounded; sending full raw history every day is not maturity.

---

## E18｜Whole-paper integrated score may be impossible to calibrate with one clean paper — MATERIAL

Because official material exposure is substantial, the maturity standard must allow a valid wider-confidence **composite calibration** when no sufficiently clean complete paper remains.

Possible evidence:

- clean/low-contamination Objective slices;
- independently scored Translation/Writing;
- separate whole-paper timing/fatigue execution;
- explicit contamination uncertainty.

This is weaker than one clean fully integrated paper and must be labeled accordingly, but the system must not burn scarce material or fabricate cleanliness merely to satisfy a “one formal full paper” ritual.

---

## Round 2 verdict

The adapted standard remains **CANDIDATE**.

E16–E18 must be added before deriving the final stage Gap Matrix.
