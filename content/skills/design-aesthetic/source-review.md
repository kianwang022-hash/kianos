- design with data;
- make complex systems simple for users;
- iterate;
- accessible design is good design;

## Durable boundaries

### Pretty is not the same as good

A design can look impressive and still:
- obscure information;
- break the task;
- confuse navigation;
- reduce accessibility;
- mismatch context.

### Minimalism is not the objective

The objective is removing low-value complexity.

Sometimes additional information, controls or detail are necessary.

### Visual hierarchy should match task hierarchy

Large / bright / central does not mean “better”.

It means:
> more attention.

Use attention deliberately.

### Typography is a system, not a font choice

Typeface matters.

But hierarchy, size, line length, weight, spacing and responsive behavior often matter more for usability.

### Color must earn a role

Color can support:
- identity;
- state;
- grouping;
- emphasis;
- mood.

Do not use more color just to make a design feel designed.

### Accessibility belongs in the first pass

Contrast, text scaling, non-color cues, input methods and error recovery should not be late-stage cleanup.

### Heuristics are lenses, not laws

Design principles help reveal risk.

They do not replace:
- user context;
- testing;
- product constraints;
- judgment.

### User research is not design-by-vote

Users are strongest at revealing:
- problems;
- behavior;
- context;
- pain;
- misunderstanding.

They are not required to design the final solution.

### A design system does not replace design judgment

Components and tokens reduce inconsistency.

They cannot decide:
- what problem matters;
- what hierarchy is correct;
- what to remove;
- how the whole experience should feel.

### Taste can be trained

Repeated exposure to high-quality references, comparison, critique and real output can improve judgment.

Do not treat taste as an innate fixed trait.

### AI-generated polish is not design quality

AI can generate many plausible variants.

The learner must still judge:
- purpose;
- coherence;
- accessibility;
- originality;
- usability;
- context fit.
