# U4 Source Review｜周尺度负荷与恢复

Status: SOURCE REVIEW COMPLETE  
Scope: U4 Week-level Load / Recovery

## Review rule

U4 does not invent a numerical readiness score. It distinguishes one bad block, one bad day, multi-day accumulated mismatch, and persistent unexplained fatigue that should leave the productivity frame.

## Accepted source set

### S1｜Sleep restriction affects cognition

Lowe CJ et al. PMID: 28757454  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28757454/

Accepted: short-term sleep restriction has measurable negative effects across multiple cognitive domains.

Learner use: several days of reduced sleep can be a different problem from one isolated low-energy session.

Boundary: no exact sleep-debt repayment formula.

### S2｜Sleep regularity is a separate signal

PMID: 41259946  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41259946/

Accepted: day-to-day sleep timing regularity is associated with multiple health/performance-relevant outcomes.

Learner use: weekly review considers amount and regularity, not total hours alone.

### S3｜Micro-breaks do not close a week-level mismatch

PMID: 36044424

Accepted: micro-breaks may improve vigor/fatigue but do not reliably restore high-demand task performance.

Learner use: repeated same-day rescue tactics should not conceal a larger load/recovery mismatch.

### S4｜Physical activity is baseline infrastructure

CDC Physical Activity Guidelines and adult health benefits.  
https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity/php/guidelines-recommendations/index.html  
https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/health-benefits/adults.html

Accepted: regular activity supports broad health; a single session has immediate benefits for some outcomes.

Boundary: U4 does not prescribe a personalized training plan.

### S5｜Persistent fatigue can require medical evaluation

NHS tiredness/fatigue; Mayo Clinic fatigue.  
https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/tiredness-and-fatigue/  
https://www.mayoclinic.org/symptoms/fatigue/basics/causes/sym-20050894

Accepted: persistent unexplained fatigue that does not improve or affects daily life can reflect medical or mental-health conditions and should not be indefinitely self-managed as a productivity issue.

Boundary: the Skill does not diagnose causes.

## Deliberately excluded

- readiness scores or HRV thresholds;
- fixed deload weeks;
- exact recovery-day ratios;
- formulas converting hours studied into recovery debt;
- diagnosing burnout/overtraining from one poor day.
