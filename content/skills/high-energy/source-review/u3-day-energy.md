# U3 Source Review｜日内精力配置

Status: SOURCE REVIEW COMPLETE  
Scope: U3 Day-level Energy Allocation

## Review rule

U3 admits evidence that changes how high-cognitive-demand work is placed across one day. It must not hard-code morning-good / afternoon-bad, chronotype identity, or caffeine as the default fix.

## Accepted source set

### S1｜Time-of-day effects are real but task-dependent

Systematic review PMID: 37589927  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37589927/

Accepted: cognition can vary by time of day; direction and magnitude depend substantially on task and methodology.

Learner use: recurring day patterns deserve attention.

Boundary: no universal best hour.

### S2｜Chronotype / synchrony effects are mixed

Systematic review PMID: 40293205  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40293205/

Accepted: most studies showed no main chronotype effect, while a meaningful subset showed better performance at an individual's optimal time for some domains.

Learner use: calibrate high/medium/low windows from repeated personal performance rather than generic chronotype advice.

Boundary: chronotype labels do not justify rigid schedules.

### S3｜Sleep restriction degrades waking performance

PMID: 28757454; PMID: 38759474.  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28757454/  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38759474/

Accepted: sleep restriction impairs sustained attention and can impair broader cognitive performance.

Learner use: day planning cannot indefinitely compensate for inadequate sleep.

### S4｜Sleep regularity

Systematic review PMID: 41259946  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41259946/

Accepted: sleep regularity is increasingly supported as a distinct dimension of sleep health, though causal evidence varies by outcome.

Learner use: protect a reasonably stable sleep-wake structure instead of optimizing daytime productivity at its expense.

Boundary: no exact personal regularity threshold.

### S5｜Caffeine timing

PMID: 36870101; PMID: 39377163.

Accepted: dose and timing influence sleep disruption.

Learner use: alertness gain must be evaluated against downstream sleep cost.

Boundary: no universal clock cutoff.

## Deliberately excluded

- universal golden hours;
- rigid chronotype scheduling;
- fixed ultradian-cycle claims;
- fixed meal timing as a cognition law;
- caffeine as the default solution to predictable low-energy windows.
