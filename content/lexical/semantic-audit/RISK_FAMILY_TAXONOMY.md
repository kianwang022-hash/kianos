# Lexical Semantic Audit Risk Families

This taxonomy supports normalized Audit Pack reporting. It does not create semantic authority beyond `CONTENT_ASSET_CONTRACT.md` and `INDEPENDENT_SEMANTIC_AUDIT_CONTRACT.md`.

Initial frozen families:

- `PRODUCTION_PATTERN`
- `FAMILIAR_MISSING_SENSE`
- `CORE_ACTIVE_MISMATCH`
- `ORDINARY_SENSE_WRONGLY_REFERENCE_ONLY`
- `RELATION_RECIPROCAL_ANCHOR`
- `FORM_CAPITALIZATION_IDENTITY`
- `FORM_SPELLING_VARIANT`
- `FORM_PRONUNCIATION`
- `OVER_CLEANUP_L3_AS_DELETE`
- `CONSTRUCTION_OR_COLLOCATION`
- `REGISTER_STANCE_VALENCE`
- `STABLE_IDENTITY_CONTINUITY`
- `OWNERSHIP_BOUNDARY`
- `OTHER_MATERIAL_SEMANTIC`

New risk families require canonical addition here rather than ad hoc per-Chat labels.
