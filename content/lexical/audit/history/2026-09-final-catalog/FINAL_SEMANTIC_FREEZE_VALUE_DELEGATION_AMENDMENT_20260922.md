# Lexical Final Semantic Freeze — Learner Value + Standing Delegation Amendment

Status: **CURRENT AMENDMENT — 2026-09-22**

Parent ruler: `FINAL_SEMANTIC_FREEZE.md`  
Scope amendment: `FINAL_SEMANTIC_FREEZE_SCOPE_AMENDMENT_20260922.md`

This amendment changes only **learner-value ranking, approval friction, and serial throughput**. It does not lower semantic correctness, owner identity, Self Attack, Independent Audit, FLOB closure, stale-hash guards, or merge permissions.

## 1. Learner-value priority

When learner attention is limited:

```text
semantic decision value
> form completeness
```

Default learner-facing priority is:

1. familiar-new meaning / important polysemy;
2. cross-word confusable / near-form / near-synonym semantic decision boundaries;
3. high-value construction, argument structure, phraseology and collocation;
4. register / stance / intensity distinctions that change interpretation or use;
5. productive lexical knowledge with real transfer value;
6. Form / Identity only when it materially changes recognition, meaning, identity or safe production.

Routine noun/verb stress alternations, ordinary pronunciation detail and regional spelling remain valid canonical Form / Identity truth but are **background support by default**. They do not automatically count as Expansion, default Depth, Repair debt, learner-facing “new knowledge”, or a reason to interrupt Kian.

## 2. Standing delegation

Kian explicitly grants standing delegation from 2026-09-22 for the full-catalog final-standard continuation.

After each exact post-Self-Attack proposal is frozen, Chat may record the batch as approved and continue without a fresh `p` when all are true:

- the proposal stays inside the frozen semantic ruler;
- evidence is sufficient for the claimed delta;
- the change is bounded and exactly enumerated;
- there is no unresolved material meaning or stable-identity ambiguity;
- fail-closed mechanical guards and Independent Audit remain intact.

The approval reference must identify this standing delegation. This delegation does **not** pre-approve unseen content: every later batch still needs its own Fresh Read, Self Attack and frozen proposal before execution.

## 3. Exceptional user Gate

A fresh user Gate is reserved for cases where the frozen ruler cannot safely decide the change, including:

- a new learning-policy category or material policy expansion;
- unresolved competing semantic interpretations;
- unresolved stable Word / Sense / Relation / Form identity choice;
- an auditor finding whose correct resolution would materially broaden learner scope beyond the frozen proposal and this delegation.

Routine background Form / Identity facts, evidence-resolved corrections, bounded audit refinements and normal new semantic relations inside the frozen ruler do not require a fresh `p`.

## 4. Throughput

One Chat may close multiple consecutive ~100-owner semantic atoms serially. Each atom remains independently accountable:

```text
100/100 Fresh Read
→ Production Self Attack
→ exact frozen proposal
→ standing delegated approval when eligible
→ fail-closed materialization
→ rebuild 7,946 FLOB
→ strict Independent Audit
→ bounded reconciliation if needed
→ merge / live-cursor advance
→ next atom
```

Do not trade audit density or semantic completeness for larger batches. Do not build a hidden future repair backlog.

## 5. BF25 interpretation under this amendment

For BF25 o2551–o2650:

- `intense ↔ intensive`, `intermediate ↔ intermittent`, and `invaluable ↔ valuable` are learner-facing semantic decision upgrades;
- `insert` noun/verb stress and `insult` noun/verb stress are canonical **background Form** facts, not important Expansion by default;
- `instalment/installment` spelling migration and `intimate` pronunciation migration are backend ownership corrections, not learner-facing Expansion.

The canonical Form facts may remain stored. This amendment changes their learner priority, not their truth.
