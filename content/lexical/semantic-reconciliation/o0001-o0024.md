## Form ownership ruling

Current Natural Owner architecture has Word owners and Relation owners but no independent `forms/` Natural Owner store. For same spelling + same Word identity + POS-conditioned pronunciation/stress, the correct Current representation is therefore a structured Word-local `record.form_identity` object.

This reconciliation authorizes one minimal owner-contract clarification:

- Word owner owns **same-Word form identity**, including POS-conditioned pronunciation/stress;
- genuine cross-Word spelling/variant relationships remain Relation-owned;
- pronunciation alone never manufactures a new Word or Sense identity.

The structured object must use `form_type = same_spelling_pos_conditioned_pronunciation`, preserve one Word owner, and expose machine-consumable variants by POS.
