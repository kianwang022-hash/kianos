## Exact proposed final semantic state for CHANGE owners

### o3356 — opt — CONSTRUCTION_BOUNDARY
Keep one ordinary choice sense. Make its argument structure explicit rather than treating all complements as interchangeable:
- **`opt for + noun/NP`** = choose a thing/option;
- **`opt to do sth`** = choose a course of action;
- **`opt in (to sth)`** = choose to participate/be included;
- **`opt out (of sth)`** = choose not to participate/be included.
`opt in/out` are construction/phrasal-verb material, not new generic senses.

### o3360 — option — PHRASEOLOGY_GAP
Keep general choice and financial option senses. Promote construction-only **`have no option but to do sth` = have no real alternative; therefore have to do it**. It belongs to the ordinary choice sense and should not be read as the financial derivative.

### o3368 — order — CORRECTNESS_GAP + CONSTRUCTION
Keep command and sequence/order branches. Repair the purchase branch:
- `sense:order:7f2e7607d60b5820` should mean **“a request for goods/services/food to be supplied”**, not narrowly “a commercial document”;
- consolidate overlapping purchase verbs `sense:order:e55bda7c9bde5349` and `sense:order:ffad803668555128` into one ordinary L1/L2 I/T branch **“request goods/food/services to be supplied; place an order”**, preserving stable aliases.
Add construction-level:
- **`in order to + infinitive` = for the purpose of doing something**;
- **`out of order` = not working correctly; not arranged in the correct sequence/order**;
- chiefly BrE informal lower use: **`out of order` = inappropriate/unacceptable/offensive behavior**.
Do not attach the broken-machine idiom to literal spatial `out`.

### o3369 — orderly — COVERAGE_GAP / CORE_ALIGNMENT

Current Core wording already claims a noun “护理员/勤务兵” while no active noun sense exists. Keep older military-messenger use lower/Reference rather than presenting it as equally current.
