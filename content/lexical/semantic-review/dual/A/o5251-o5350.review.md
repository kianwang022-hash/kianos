### o5305 — virtue — PHRASEOLOGY / SENSE_ATTACHMENT
Move `by virtue of` off moral-goodness `sense:virtue:7eb217044b755cfa`. Define the construction **`by virtue of sth` = because of / as a result of / by reason of sth**, often formal. Keep moral virtue and good-quality senses separate.

### o5310 — visit — SEMANTIC_CORRECTION / REGIONAL
Refine mainly-AmE `visit with sb`: **spend time talking/socializing with someone**, not simply “stay with someone as a guest”. Keep transitive `visit a friend/place` and noun `a visit` separate.
### o5320 — void — SENSE_ATTACHMENT
Move `void of` off noun `sense:void:9a46b25fefd654cb` “empty area/feeling of emptiness”. Attach it to adjective `sense:void:81aed3effde553c1`: **`be void of sth` = completely lacking/devoid of sth**. Keep legal `void` separate.

### o5338 — wake — PHRASEOLOGY / SENSE_ATTACHMENT
Move `in the wake of sth` off literal ship-wave `sense:wake:b3df0c5f2e4652f0`. Keep the literal noun for the track/waves behind a moving vessel, and attach the figurative construction **`in the wake of sth` = following as a consequence/aftermath of sth** to phraseology.

### o5342 — wander — TRANSITIVITY_CORRECTION
Refine movement `sense:wander:43d1f637316856f3` to **I/T** where current standard use permits `wander through/around a place` and bare route/location objects such as `wander the streets`. Current `vi + through/around` metadata conflicts with the attached transitive example.

### o5343 — want — TRANSITIVITY / GOVERNING_PATTERN
