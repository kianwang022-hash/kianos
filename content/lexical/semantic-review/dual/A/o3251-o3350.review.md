## Exact proposed final semantic state for CHANGE owners

### o3257 — note — COVERAGE_GAP
Keep existing written-note, short-message, musical-note, tone/quality, notice and write-down senses. Add **NEW noun sense, L1/L2, chiefly BrE: “a piece of paper money; banknote”**, e.g. `a £20 note`. Do not merge with written-message `note`.

### o3259 — nothing — PHRASEOLOGY_GAP / SENSE_ATTACHMENT
Keep pronoun/noun/adverb senses. Move lexicalized phrases out of bare pronoun collocation storage:
- **`nothing but X` = only X / no more than X**;
- **`have/be nothing to do with sb/sth` = have no connection/involvement with sb/sth**;
- where the person-focused use is retained, **`it's nothing to do with sb` = it is not that person's business/concern**.
These are construction-level meanings, not extra core senses.

### o3272 — nowhere — PHRASEOLOGY_GAP
Keep literal no-place adverb and remote/insignificant-place noun. Add construction-only:
- **`get/go nowhere` = fail to make progress or achieve a result**;
- **`nowhere near X` = far from X / much less than X**.
`in the middle of nowhere` remains on the remote-place noun branch.

### o3277 — number — CONSTRUCTION_BOUNDARY
Keep numeral, amount/total, identification-number and verb senses; preserve existing `number ↔ amount` countability Relation.
Add explicit phrase boundary:
- **`a number of + plural count noun` = several/many**;
- **`the number of + plural count noun` = the total/count**.
Do not collapse these into one construction. Agreement follows the intended phrase structure/context; teach the semantic contrast first, not a brittle one-line agreement rule.

### o3280 — nurse — CORRECTNESS_GAP / CONSTRUCTION
Keep nurse-person, care-for, long-held-feeling and breastfeeding branches. Fix breastfeeding branch `sense:nurse:26845096841e5a5f`:
- transitive **`nurse a baby` = breastfeed a baby**;
- intransitive subject-baby use **`the baby nurses / nurses at the breast` = drinks breast milk**.
Current definition “feed a baby from the breast” combined with only `vi. + at` is internally inconsistent. Preserve both I/T patterns under one breastfeeding sense.

### o3294 — object — DECISION_RELEVANT_FORM

### o3299 — obscure — CORRECTNESS_GAP / SENSE_SPLIT

### o3301 — observe — COVERAGE/GRAMMAR_CORRECTION

Current narrow `vi. + on/upon` pattern is not the main modern learner pattern. `observe on/upon sth` may remain Reference/formal if independently supported.

### o3302 — obsession — SCOPE_CORRECTION

### o3336 — once — PHRASEOLOGY_GAP

### o3337 — one — PHRASEOLOGY / ATTACHMENT

Lower-priority uses such as `get onto a new subject`, `hold onto`, or `get onto the plumber` should be handled only if their owning construction/phrasal-verb layer requires them; do not inflate this Word owner.

### o3350 — opinion — SCOPE_CORRECTION
