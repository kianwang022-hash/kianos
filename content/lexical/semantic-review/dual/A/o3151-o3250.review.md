### o3162 + o3200 — must ↔ need — DECISION_GAP / CONSTRUCTION_BOUNDARY
Keep existing modal/main-verb senses. Add:
- on `must`: **`must not / mustn't do sth` = it is prohibited / not permitted**;
- on modal `need`: **`need not / needn't do sth` = it is not necessary**.
Add one reciprocal learner-facing Relation Natural Owner anchored to modal `sense:must:a12c20ee1fdf503e` and auxiliary `sense:need:bc81e25cfae65772`.
Boundary: **`mustn't` = do not do it; `needn't` = you may do it, but it is unnecessary**. Do not collapse prohibition into absence of obligation. Existing inference `must be / must have + pp` remains separate.

### o3163 — mute — COVERAGE_GAP + REGISTER_FORM_GAP
Keep adjective silent/not speaking and verb make quieter/silent. Reactivate stable `sense:mute:855f51c881745276` as an L2 noun: **a device/control used to reduce or stop sound, especially a musical-instrument mute; electronic `mute/mute button` is the same productive sound-suppression family**.
For adjective `mute`, retain ordinary neutral uses such as `remain mute / mute admiration`, but explicitly mark the use meaning **“unable to speak” about a person as old-fashioned and offensive**. Deprecated noun referring to a person who cannot speak remains Reference and must not be reactivated.
