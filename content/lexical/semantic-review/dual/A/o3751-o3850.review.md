### o3779 — principle — PHRASEOLOGY_GAP

Do not split these into new generic noun senses.

### o3780 — print — PHRASEOLOGY_GAP
Keep print verb and printed-text/impression nouns. Expose lexicalized constructions:
- **`in print` = currently available in printed/publication form**;
- **`out of print` = no longer being printed/published and therefore not normally available new**;
- **`the small/fine print` = detailed conditions or restrictions, often less noticeable than the main message**.
These are not equivalent to generic “printed text”.

### o3787 — privilege — COVERAGE_GAP
